Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: January 2025

How to Request Data Deletion

We respect your right to control your personal data. If you would like to request the deletion of your data from AI Newspaper, please follow the instructions below.

To request data deletion, send an email to:

dobryden.zpravy@gmail.com

What to Include in Your Request

Please include the following information in your email:

  1. Subject line: "Data Deletion Request"
  2. Your identification: Any information that helps us identify your data (e.g., email address, username, or account details if applicable)
  3. Specific request: Whether you want all your data deleted or only specific data

What Happens Next

After we receive your request:

  • We will acknowledge your request within 48 hours
  • We will verify your identity to protect your data from unauthorized deletion requests
  • We will process your deletion request within 30 days
  • You will receive confirmation once your data has been deleted

What Data Will Be Deleted

Upon your request, we will delete:

  • Any personal information associated with your account or usage
  • Usage logs and analytics data linked to you
  • Any content you have submitted through our service

Data We May Retain

Please note that we may retain certain data as required by law or for legitimate business purposes, such as:

  • Records required for legal compliance
  • Anonymized or aggregated data that cannot identify you
  • Data necessary to prevent fraud or protect our legal rights

Automatic Data Deletion

For your information, our system automatically deletes processed content after 30 days. This includes any articles, images, audio, or video content generated through our service.
